# How Silmari Handles Your Data

This page says, in plain words, where your data lives and who can see it.

## What stays on your Mac

- Your browsing, history, bookmarks and saved passwords stay on your Mac. Silmari never sends them to us.
- Your files stay on your Mac. The assistant can reach a file only through its file tools.
- The assistant may use two folders freely: `~/zigBrowser-files` and `Documents/Silmari Artifacts`.
- Any other folder needs your permission first. Silmari asks in a card, and you choose Allow or Deny.

## What goes to the cloud

- Your chats with the assistant are stored in the cloud.
- Each chat is encrypted with a key that belongs to you alone.
- Your assistant runs in its own private cloud computer (a "Machine"). No other user shares it.
- The assistant uses your own Claude account. Your Claude sign-in is stored encrypted and is never written to logs.
- Files reach the cloud only when the assistant reads them with your permission, as described above.

## Where it is stored

All cloud storage and every Machine are in the United States. Nothing is stored in another country.

## Who can see it

- Silmari staff cannot read your chats, because they are encrypted for you alone.
- The owner of Silmari sees only account details: your email, when you joined, when you last used Silmari, and how much you used it.
- We do not sell or share your data. We show no ads, and we do not track you.

## Crash reports

Crash reports are off unless you turn them on. A report holds only the crash location in the code, the Silmari version, the macOS version and the type of Mac. It never holds pages, files or personal data.

## Health information

Silmari is not covered by a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. Do not enter protected health information into Silmari.

## Export your chats

You can export your chats at any time from the assistant panel in Silmari.

## Delete your data

To delete your data, email privacy@silmari.ai from the address you signed up with. We delete your account, your devices, your Machine, your encrypted chats and your crash reports within 30 days. A delete button in Silmari comes later.
