# Silmari Privacy Policy

This is the owner-approved draft. It is written in plain English and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

## Who we are

Silmari is a Mac web browser with an AI assistant. It is offered free to a small family group. Contact: privacy@silmari.ai.

## What we collect

- Your email address, so you can sign in.
- When you joined and when you last used Silmari.
- How much you used the assistant's cloud computer, so we can see our costs.
- Your chats with the assistant, stored encrypted for you alone. We cannot read them.
- Crash reports, only if you turn them on. They are off unless you choose otherwise.

## What we do not collect

- Your browsing, history, bookmarks and saved passwords. They stay on your Mac.
- Your files, unless the assistant reads them with your permission.
- Any page content, file or personal data in crash reports.

## Your Claude account

The assistant uses your own Claude account. It runs in your own private cloud computer. Your Claude sign-in is stored encrypted and is never written to logs.

## Where your data is stored

All cloud data and cloud computers are in the United States.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. We show no ads and use no tracking.

## Health information

Silmari is not covered by a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. Do not enter protected health information.

## Export and deletion

You can export your chats from the assistant panel. To delete your data, email privacy@silmari.ai. We delete it within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will tell you in Silmari or by email before the change takes effect.

More detail: [How Silmari Handles Your Data](DATA.md).
